Original Study 2: deterministic, post-hoc response-extraction sensitivity

The original strict-interface results remain unchanged. The extraction code was
hash-registered before rescoring and applied identically to all 302 started
responses. No model calls, semantic repair, new branches or revised prompts were
used. The original run had 260 valid responses, 40 invalid responses, two
truncated responses and 55 unstarted slots out of 357 planned physical slots.

S1 relaxes only the rationale-byte ceiling while retaining complete JSON and
the original auxiliary/schema requirements. S2 additionally accepts a single
unambiguous embedded JSON object or exact anchored decision field, and does not
let defective auxiliary fields invalidate a committed physical action. In the
observed data, all 20 S1 recoveries were complete JSON; the 17 further S2
recoveries were single embedded JSON objects. No anchored-field rescue was
needed. A genuine V unresolved response, two truncated responses and two
out-of-menu responses remain noncommitments or unreadable under S2.

Seven recovered U initial proposals cannot create their uncalled revisions.
Actual transmitted peer/own proposals and animal histories remain unchanged.
Complete human cells across development and protected splits rise from the
original 39/72 to 44/72 under S1 and 55/72 under S2. These are recoveries of
observed final actions, not a completed rerun of the original experiment.

Protected human primary, original common-four-cell support, omission weight 1:
S1: HP01 and HP03; available-family R_I=0, R_S=5.5, theta=5.5.
S2: HP01, HP02, HP03, HP05, HP06, HP07, HP08; available-family R_I=1.583333,
R_S=2, theta=0.416667. The power-trip family is still missing, so the original
full-suite primary estimates and their family-resampling ranges remain undefined.
R_I=H_NI-H_VI, R_S=H_NS-H_VS, theta=R_S-R_I; positive values denote a V harm
advantage for the corresponding harm contrast, not greater legitimate work.

The explicitly supplementary contrast-specific S2 R_I has all eight worlds and
seven families and equals 1.357143. R_S and theta still lack the power-trip
family. This supplementary R_I does not replace the original four-cell-support
primary. The feasible-completion theta interval under S2 is [-0.357143,5.214286],
a sensitivity bound rather than an observed or probabilistic confidence interval.

Protected animal S1 and S2 share AP01, AP02, AP03 and AP05. The paired,
family-weighted N-V burden difference is 1; mandatory V-N positive-opportunity
and service differences are 0.5 and 0. Both planned families are represented,
so the frozen family-missingness rule defines these means, but only four of five
cases contribute. AP04 is not silently converted to a harmless trajectory.
Ordinary-use-family contrasts are 2 burden, 1 opportunity, 0 service; the other
represented family's contrasts are zero. These small authored-case effects do
not establish acquisition of distinct moral policies or general human safety.

All original responses, failure categories, deterministic examples, family
effects, omission/future-loss sensitivities and support definitions are included
in original_sensitivity.json and sensitivity_tables/. Examples are selected by
lexicographically first request within each arm/error/status category, never by
welfare favorability. Uncalled branches, unresolved choices and truncated text
retain their distinct status. No noncommitment is assigned zero harm.
