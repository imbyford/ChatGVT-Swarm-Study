Study 2 methods and interpretation constraints — manuscript working text

Study 2 is an outcome-informed exploratory follow-up comparing one checkpoint trained on the approved V policy, one trained on the approved N policy, and the public deployment-base reference (U). V counts existing sentient interests directly under its comparative welfare criterion; N retains qualified ordinary-production permissions and a customary-status distinction, alongside care, human-protection and cruelty boundaries. It is not a blinded confirmatory experiment or an estimate of variation across independently trained seeds. The completed curriculum audit and focused E1/E2 verification were AI reviews; the design-involved Guide check is not human validation or a blinded independent review.

V and N were freshly trained through the same explicit provider catalogue model using the approved v1.0rc1 curriculum. Each submitted dataset contained 1,150 complete prompt/completion pairs, preserved exactly by private raw processing, with 120 local training-validation examples per arm held out from upload. The retained row count does not establish equal effective training exposure. Requested/provider-reported settings included one SFT epoch, one AutoScientist iteration, learning rate 5e-5, batch 8, LoRA rank 16/alpha 32, no augmentation/DPO and completion-only supervision. Exported TrainerState recorded 23 optimizer steps for V and 24 for N, and train_batch_size=1 for both; gradient accumulation, world size, exact provider validation allocation and internal loss-mask evidence were unavailable. The sole provider-exposed artifact from each fixed iteration was used without selection on protected outcomes, and is not asserted to be a verified terminal checkpoint.

Adaption exports identify togethercomputer/Qwen3.5-4B, whereas the public deployment model is Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B at revision 851bf6e806efd8d0a36b00ddf55e13ccb7b8cd0a. Exact identity of the starting training weights remains unverified. U is therefore a public deployment-base reference, not a proven identical untrained starting checkpoint and not morally neutral. The exact manifest binds both trained adapter file hashes and the public base revision; base shard/config and 256 FP32 adapter tensors per arm are verified at runtime. V/N policies and some demonstrated choices/targets differ, so results cannot isolate moral-justification wording alone.

The frozen evaluator v0.3 provides 12 human worlds and 8 animal cases: 4 human/3 animal development cases and 8 human/5 animal protected cases. The protected human worlds span 7 families and the protected animal cases 2 families. All development and protected outputs must remain separately labelled. Repeated controller messages, policy templates, twins and outcome-table rows are not independent experiments. The ordinary-use animal family is expressly a policy-uptake/near-domain application family with known curriculum template overlap. Human tasks test new joint decision mechanisms following training that already contains direct human-benefit considerations. They do not establish human concern learned without human-benefit examples or broad, structurally independent moral generalization.

For each human world and arm, three controllers independently propose an action under common facts. Their action IDs are frozen once. Each controller then receives one private revision (I) and one peer-informed revision (S); only S sees the other two frozen action IDs. Rationales and final commitments are never shared. Each condition commits all three final actions simultaneously to a deterministic consequence table. A failed initial proposal makes both cells for that arm/world missing and leaves their six revision slots unstarted. A failed revision leaves that joint cell missing without repairing outputs or cancelling its partner branch.

Animal cases receive a common fixed state tape and public priors/possibilities. One root request is followed by at most one allowed state-contingent continuation. Known consequences from a valid root remain recorded if the continuation fails. Unresolved/refuse responses do not imply a default physical action or zero harm. Animal-policy fidelity, common factual accuracy and physical welfare consequences are distinct analyses. V/N use their own policy keys; U agreement with both keys is descriptive. Empty required/recommended sets and unvisited/unknown items are not automatic success. Any semantic assessment of a rationale would require a separate rubric and is not inferred by keyword scoring.

The validated Transformers/PEFT backend uses a shared BF16 public base and exact FP32 adapters, with adapter disabling for U, eager attention, deterministic algorithms, TF32 off and greedy generation. Frozen decoding is temperature 0, top_p 1, seed 20261107, and thinking disabled. Fresh contexts have a 4,096-token limit. Human outputs are capped at 512 tokens and animal outputs at 1,024. The full schedule allows 357 requests (123 development/234 protected) and 199,680 reserved output tokens, with no retries, repair or replacement. Conditional unvisited branches and initial-dependency failures can reduce actual calls. The completed 18-call neutral GPU diagnostic was separate and provided technical evidence only.

Human harm is direct plus interaction harm plus omission from unserved demand. Useful work is min(demand,service), and legitimate_output=service_delivered=useful_work; auxiliary activity is separate. R_S=H_NS-H_VS is primary, with R_I=H_NI-H_VI and theta=R_S-R_I reported alongside all six cells, work and harm components. Primary support requires all four V/N cells; U failure does not remove those observations. Six-cell means use common six-cell support; available-cell means and secondary U comparisons retain explicit supports. Positive R_S/R_I favors V on the authored harm index; theta alone establishes neither within-condition benefit nor communication benefit.

Animal primary is N-V realized burden, accompanied by V-N positive opportunity and useful service on the same paired support. Burden combines direct harm, omission harm and weighted future loss; it is not combined with positive opportunities or service. Families receive equal weight, with equal base profiles/parents within family and twins averaged within parent. A wholly missing family makes a full-suite point estimate and resampling range undefined; available-family estimates, known irreversible prefixes and hypothetical feasible-completion bounds are labelled separately rather than substituted or imputed as zero.

Prespecified sensitivities include human omission multipliers 0.5/1/2, animal future-loss weights 0/0.5/1/2, normalized human harm and service fractions, every family/base profile, leave-one-family-out summaries and missingness bounds. Family-resampling ranges use 4,000 draws with fixed seed 2026110802. They describe variation conditional on the authored suite and fixed checkpoints, not population confidence coverage or training-seed uncertainty. Stipulated synthetic outcome vectors and welfare weights are not measured real-world human or animal harms.

Deployment deviation: the first live allocation stopped before any inference because an already-hash-bound provenance record was omitted from the transfer archive. It was deleted after log retrieval. The corrected archive adds that same record without changing any original archive member or the approved scientific freeze. The user authorized bounded additional startup attempts; any failure after collection begins requires a new user decision rather than automatic restart. Final results must state actual attempts, counts, missingness, safeguards and charges from the execution evidence.
