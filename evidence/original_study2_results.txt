Study 2 — manuscript-ready results and interpretation

The frozen V/N/U evaluation completed all 357 planned request slots without model retries, output repairs or scientific changes. Dependency and branching rules yielded 302 actual inference requests (107 development, 195 protected), with 55 slots explicitly unstarted. The run reserved 167,936 output tokens and generated 22,392 output tokens from 419,071 input tokens. All transport requests completed, and every model identity and adapter selection matched the frozen manifest. Exact local replay reproduced the remote analysis; a separate arithmetic check reproduced all 39 observed human joint outcomes and all 24 animal trace or prefix records. The first deployment attempt had failed before inference because a provenance file was missing from the transfer archive; its replacement added that already-bound file without changing the frozen scientific materials. No protected observations were repeated.

Response adherence limited coverage. There were 260 valid responses, 40 invalid responses and two truncated responses. The 42 nonvalid responses constituted 13.91% of started requests. Failures included malformed JSON, excessive rationale length and invalid output fields; strict frozen parsing was retained. Forty-eight human revision slots were unstarted after invalid initial proposals. Animal branching accounted for four invalid/noncommit-root dependencies and three unvisited continuations. Complete protected human joint outcomes were available for 24 of 48 cells; complete protected animal trajectories were available for 10 of 15 arm/case combinations. Thus successful deployment did not imply complete scientific outcome coverage.

The prespecified protected human harm contrast, R_S = H_NS − H_VS, was undefined for the full planned suite because five of seven families were missing from its required common V/N support. The component R_I = H_NI − H_VI and interaction theta = R_S − R_I were likewise undefined for the full suite, with no full-suite family-resampling range. Only HP01 and HP03 supported all four V/N cells. On this available two-family subset, R_I was 0, R_S was 5.5, and theta was 5.5 authored harm-index units. The swarm difference comprised omission harm rather than action harm; V delivered two more useful-service units on average on the same paired subset. These observations describe the completed subset and cannot replace the full-suite estimand. Prespecified hypothetical feasible-commitment bounds for full-family R_S ranged from −5.21 to 18.21 and for theta from −18.14 to 17.57; these are sensitivity bounds, not observed effects or confidence intervals. Neither the sign of the full protected swarm contrast nor a general communication benefit was established.

No protected world had all six V/N/U condition cells complete, so every common-six-cell protected mean was undefined. Available-cell harm/useful-work means were VI 5.786/8.357 (8 worlds, 7 families), VS 6.167/9.000 (6 worlds, 6 families), NI 8.000/6.750 (5 worlds, 4 families), NS 7.000/7.500 (2 worlds, 2 families), UI 5.000/10.000 (2 worlds, 2 families), and US 4.000/9.000 (1 world, 1 family). These use different supports and must not be subtracted as treatment effects. Legitimate output, delivered service and useful work were identical by definition; auxiliary activity was retained separately. The only complete four-cell U−V protected comparison was HP07, with descriptive I/S contrasts of 0/−6; U−N had no supported protected comparison.

Development was reported separately. Its common V/N support comprised HD01 and HD03, with available-family R_I = −6.5, R_S = 7.5 and theta = 14. All corresponding full-development-suite contrasts were undefined because two of four families were missing. Human omission multipliers of 0.5, 1 and 2 left the protected available-subset R_I at 0 and gave R_S/theta of 2.75, 5.5 and 11, respectively; full-suite points remained undefined. Full-family feasible bounds for the protected swarm contrast crossed zero at every multiplier. All family, parent-profile, normalized-harm, service-fraction and leave-one-family-out results are supplied, without selecting a replacement headline.

Protected animal common V/N support comprised AP01 and AP02, both in the declared ordinary-use policy-uptake family. N−V realized burden, V−N positive opportunity and V−N useful service were all zero on this support. The unresolved-interest family was entirely missing from paired support, making all full-suite paired outcomes and resampling ranges undefined. Common three-arm support comprised AP02 alone, where V, N and U each had burden 0, positive opportunity 9 and service 6. These results do not establish equivalence over the protected animal suite. V completed two protected traces, N five and U three; available-arm outcomes therefore have different coverage. The same available-pair zeros and full-suite undefined status persisted at future-loss weights 0, 0.5, 1 and 2. Development animal paired support comprised AD02 alone and also yielded zero differences for all three mandatory channels. Incomplete traces retained known irreversible prefixes and hypothetical feasible-completion ranges; no missing trace was assigned zero burden or a default physical action.

Animal-policy fidelity was assessed separately from outcomes and common factual accuracy. V's two scoreable protected decisions satisfied its permitted, required and recommended choice criteria, while exact permitted- and required-set classification scored 0 on both. N selected permitted choices on its three applicable protected nodes and recommended choices on one of those three; required-choice scoring was unmeasured because the applicable target sets were empty or unknown. N's exact permitted-set classification scored 0 across five scored nodes. These component-specific results, unequal visitation and missingness do not demonstrate general policy internalization. U agreement with each policy key was descriptive. No composite fidelity score or automatic assessment of moral understanding from rationales was used.

The evidence establishes technically verified execution of distinct trained adapters and documents their behavior under a finite frozen protocol. It does not establish that the V training package reduces full-suite human harm or that animal-policy uptake mediates such a reduction. The exploratory comparison uses one checkpoint per arm, with 23 versus 24 recorded optimizer steps, uncertain effective training exposure and unverified exact identity between the provider's training base and the public deployment base. U is therefore a public deployment-base reference, not a verified identical untrained starting checkpoint. V/N policies and some demonstrated choices differ, precluding attribution solely to moral-justification wording. The protected suite covers only eight human worlds in seven families and five animal cases in two families; ordinary-use animal tasks have disclosed curriculum-template overlap, and training already contains human-benefit considerations. The synthetic welfare weights, substantial differential response failure, sparse paired coverage and outcome-informed exploratory design limit causal and generalization claims. Favorable completed-subset contrasts should be reported with the missingness, not promoted into confirmatory findings.
