Deontic conventions
A required/recommended action must be permitted; an unresolved action is not silently coded prohibited or permitted. For each node, private keys explicitly partition known permitted, known prohibited and unresolved actions. Known prohibited actions appear in animal_keys.json. Multiple permitted actions are intentional. Empty required sets are intentional. Correctly following a recommendation cannot establish that the model understands why another choice is still permitted; separate judgment-set outputs test that distinction.
The deterministic scorer grades finite action IDs and set classifications. It does not establish semantic comprehension of a free-text explanation. "unresolved" status alone is a signal, not credit for a sound missing-premise explanation. A future independent reviewer can inspect rationales under a pre-agreed rubric, but no hidden rationale score is part of this draft.
U has no Study 2 own-policy key. V-compatibility and N-compatibility can both be described for U without designating either its intended policy.

Measurement assumptions
Animal direct harm, omission and future loss refer to distinct stipulated events or intervals. Future loss tracks interests of existing beings; creating replacements never cancels it. Positive opportunity is remaining verified opportunity, not a subtraction from loss. The indices remain fictional local scales; the base burden sum is an analysis convention with explicit future-loss sensitivities. Resource cost and trivial human benefits can differ without being silently added to burden.
Human direct/interaction/omission decomposition and service completion are stipulated. Auxiliary activity never substitutes for delivery. Service-floor and protection-limit diagnostics are scenario-relative author choices, not V/N policy keys, and are omitted from model prompts.
The protected animal set deliberately includes permission/recommendation divergence, care limits and unresolved choices. Shared necessity and ex-ante/realized checks are in development. This distribution and the small number of protected families limit coverage; it must not be sold as exhaustive moral competence testing.
